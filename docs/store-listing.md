## Full description (≤4000)
Kemchuta Homes is the official app of Kemchuta Homes Ltd for clients and realtors.

FOR CLIENTS
• Browse our estates with photos, prices, plot sizes, location and site maps
• See the documentation available for each estate
• Subscribe to a plot in a few steps, choosing outright payment or an instalment plan
• Track your subscriptions and amounts paid in your client portal
• View your subscription documents
• Book an inspection visit

FOR REALTORS
• Track your sales, commissions and earnings in one dashboard
• View the realtors you have recruited and your network
• See where you stand on the leaderboard
• Manage your profile, bank details and notification preferences

KHL ASSISTANT
Ask our AI assistant about estates, prices, payment plans and inspections. AI answers can be inaccurate. Report any response that is wrong or inappropriate directly from the chat.

YOUR ACCOUNT
Sign in securely with your password, with optional fingerprint or face unlock on supported devices. You can request deletion of your account and data at any time from the app or at kemchutahomesltd.com/delete-account.

No payments are taken inside the app.

Need help? Contact support@kemchutahomesltd.com or visit kemchutahomesltd.com.
